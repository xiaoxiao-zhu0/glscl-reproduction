# Slide QA checklist

Check the rendered deck at the target aspect ratio and at least one normal presentation scale.

## Content

- Every slide has one clear page job and takeaway; adjacent pages advance the argument rather than merely changing topic.
- The title's promised operation or comparison is demonstrated by visible steps/details. The audience can follow the decisive input, criterion/change and outcome where relevant; terminology and a formula alone do not replace the needed explanation. Source-backed intermediate detail stays on-page when needed for the requested audience and density.
- Each evidence focus identifies the relevant source section, figure/table or value; a filename alone is not a content check.
- Claims, metrics, units, formulas, citations, and table values match the source.
- Headline results use the stated protocol and comparable baseline conditions; report-only evidence is not described as independently verified raw data. Material source conflicts, missing evidence and contribution boundaries are resolved or explicitly disclosed, not lost during summary/recovery. Recheck sources affected by version or scope changes.
- No placeholder, generated image, or decorative label implies unsupported evidence.
- The opening establishes the problem and the ending states the contribution and limits.
- The selected story reflects both the whole-project analysis and the user's requested scope/emphasis; unresolved intent questions are not treated as answered.

## Visuals

- No text, equation, table, legend, or diagram is clipped or overlaps another object.
- The largest element is the intended focus; supporting elements are visibly secondary.
- Text is readable from a projected screen, including Chinese glyphs and chart labels.
- Colors meet contrast needs and encode the same meaning across slides.
- Figures use consistent crop, stroke, marker, and annotation conventions.
- Generated images support the slide's claim, have no accidental labels or watermarks, and leave the intended text-safe area clear.
- Each content-page visual communicates a source-backed operation, relationship, comparison or result. Generic topical decoration is not counted as an explanation.
- Before generation, the asset brief identifies the visible details that distinguish the project's operation; prompt-review notes explain source grounding and the generated/native/data split. Consequential unknowns are resolved or the affected claim is withheld; assumptions are not reported as user-confirmed facts.
- On the composed page, the critical state, boundary or relationship is visible at presentation scale. Replacing the operation with a neighboring method should require changing that explanatory layer. Labels identify visible relationships instead of asserting missing ones.
- Representation and detail level serve the page's argument; increasing resolution or asset count does not count as fixing shallow analysis.
- For component compositions, labels/connectors point to the intended objects or states, local enlargements identify their source region, and shared identity, scale and viewpoint are coherent. Inspect cutout edges/backgrounds and detail readability in the assembled slide. A gallery of good assets does not validate the page.
- When using a whole-page design reference, compare it with the actual reconstructed PPTX render: hierarchy, density, scale and useful visual detail survive reconstruction, while generated text/data and incorrect relationships are removed or rebuilt from sources. A concept image is not a final preview, and the full concept behind duplicate editable labels is not an editable reconstruction.
- Comparisons use consistent conditions and display conventions. Apply domain-specific evidence checks from the relevant conditional reference; distinguish illustration from actual results.
- If motion is used, the reveal order matches the speaker's explanation and the first/static frame remains understandable.
- Density matches the current dialogue and intended reading context. Compact pages retain requested technical content with clear hierarchy; spacious pages are not imposed as a universal aesthetic. Split, simplify or move detail into notes only when consistent with the user's page/content constraints.
- Images correspond visibly to the adjacent explanation and the user's intended meaning. After negative image feedback, review the changed subject, abstraction, viewpoint or composition rather than declaring success from technical accuracy alone.

## Export

- PDF pages preserve fonts, formulas, SVGs, Mermaid diagrams, and image quality.
- PPTX export, if requested, has been opened or rendered for a second inspection.
- For native PPTX, main titles/body/labels are actual editable objects, with declared tables/charts checked in the exported file. A full-page picture plus an editable page number does not pass. When an editing app is available, select/change representative text and a chart/table in a temporary copy; otherwise report structural inspection and its limits rather than claiming an interactive edit test.
- Final previews were rendered from the delivered PPTX, not just from a different Slidev/PDF source. Record exceptions when a suitable PPTX renderer is unavailable; the rendering gate remains incomplete.
- The source remains runnable after export and does not depend on undeclared remote assets.
- New/revised plans use `sha256-v1` review tracking. Current file/plan snapshots match inspected page and export reviews; edits invalidate old approvals.
- Any known export limitation is recorded for the user.
- Animation is reported as preserved, simulated with stepped slides, or lost during export; do not leave this ambiguous.
